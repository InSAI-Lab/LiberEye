This page displays planned outputs without connecting to a wristband. D1-D4 encode approach distance and W1-W3 encode warning actions. These states do not measure cognitive load or physical motor response.

Mechanism and validation

  • The paper's EPMC policy selects W3 for a supervisory stop and otherwise uses cue priority. See the policy implementation.
  • Urgent cues or danger suppress optional scene descriptions. See the coordination tests.
  • This page uses the legacy coordinator. Scenario buttons replay synthetic inputs; uploads use the configured perception pipeline.
